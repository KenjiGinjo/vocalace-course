会上不知道从哪接。一开口又像在翻译：*I think you are right. I also want to add…* 想插一句，直接说 *Wait* 或 *But*，气氛会紧。这课三句就够：同意并接下去，顺着补一点，打断之前先问一句。

*Could you* 的问句往上，见 [6.2.1](/6-dialogue/6-2/could/) 和 [3.2.1](/3-sentences/3-2/contour/)。*to* 不强调时是 /tə/。*Right* 当「我同意」见 [7.3.1](/7-expressions/7-3/yeah/)。这课用另外三句开口。

没有整句录音。think [🔊](/audio/words/think.mp3)、good [🔊](/audio/words/good.mp3)、just [🔊](/audio/words/just.mp3)、here [🔊](/audio/words/here.mp3) 只对单词。

这课目标：*Absolutely. And I think…* *Just to add to that…* *Could I jump in here?* 或 *Sorry to interrupt. I have a quick question.*

## 口型

| 你要做 | 先说 | 不要先说 |
|--------|------|----------|
| 同意，并接自己的一句 | Absolutely. And I think… | Yes, I think so. 然后停 |
| 同意一个具体的点 | That's a good point. We should also consider… | I agree with you. 当作整句 |
| 顺着补信息 | Just to add to that, | 只有一个 Also |
| 会快结束，还有一点 | One more thing worth mentioning, | 抢在别人句中 |
| 对方还在说，你有一句短的 | Sorry to interrupt. I have a quick question. | Wait. / But. |
| 问能不能插进来 | Could I jump in here? | Excuse me, may I speak? |

*Just* 把补充说软。你是在接着讲，不是在纠正。*quick* 是在说这句不长。

*Sorry to interrupt* 说完停一下，再问问题。不停的话，听起来像在催。

## 对比词

| 说法 | 音 |
|------|----|
| Absolutely | /ˈæbsəluːtli/ |
| consider | /kənˈsɪdɚ/ |
| Just to add to that | /dʒʌst tə æd tə ðæt/ |
| worth | /wɝːθ/ |
| interrupt | /ˌɪntəˈrʌpt/ |
| Could I jump in here? | /kʊd aɪ dʒʌmp ɪn hɪr/ |

*Absolutely* 四个音节，重音在第一拍 /æb/，嘴角向两侧，见 [1.1.2](/1-phonetics/1-1/ae-vs-e/)。后面三拍轻。

*consider* 重音在第二拍，词尾 /ɚ/，见 [1.2.3](/1-phonetics/1-2/er-weak/)。*worth* 是 /wɝːθ/，见 [1.2.2](/1-phonetics/1-2/er/) 和 [1.5.1](/1-phonetics/1-5/th-s/)。*mentioning* 是 /ˈmɛnʃənɪŋ/。

两个 *to* 都是 /tə/。重音在 *add*。just [🔊](/audio/words/just.mp3) /dʒʌst/。

*interrupt* 重音在最后一拍 *rupt*。*Could I jump in here?* 句尾往上。here [🔊](/audio/words/here.mp3) /hɪr/。*good* 是 /ɡʊd/。good [🔊](/audio/words/good.mp3)。think [🔊](/audio/words/think.mp3) /θɪŋk/。

## 放进句子

**句 1：**

> **Could I** jump in? **Just to add** to that, the **deadline** might be an issue.  
> /kʊd aɪ dʒʌmp ɪn ‖ dʒʌst tə æd tə ðæt ðə ˈdɛdlaɪn maɪt bi ən ˈɪʃuː/  
> 我插一句。补充一下，截止日期可能是个问题。

先问，再补。*Could I* 往上。*deadline* 清楚。

**句 2：**

> **Absolutely.** That's a **good point.** And one more thing **worth mentioning** is the **budget**.  
> /ˈæbsəluːtli ‖ ðæts ə ɡʊd pɔɪnt ‖ wɝːθ ˈmɛnʃənɪŋ ɪz ðə ˈbʌdʒɪt/  
> 对。这一点很好。还有一件要提的是预算。

*point* 是 /pɔɪnt/。*budget* 是 /ˈbʌdʒɪt/。

## 常见错误

**1. Absolutely 重音在后面**  
第一拍 *ABS* 说清楚，后面快过。不要把力气放在 *lute*。

**2. interrupt 后面马上追问**  
*Sorry to interrupt* 说完停一下。再开始 *I have a quick question.*

**3. to 读成 /tuː/**  
*just to add to that* 里两个 *to* 都轻。整块滑过去，重音只留在 *add*。

## 练习

### A｜跟读（约 4 分钟）

下面三句各说两遍，连着录一遍。回听 *Absolutely* 的重音，以及 *jump in* 往不往上。

- Absolutely. And I think we need more time.
- Just to add to that, the cost is also a factor.
- Could I jump in? I have a quick question.

过关：能指出重音、句尾，或 *to* 有没有读满。

### B｜开口（约 4 分钟）

同事 Alex 的话印在下面。他说完，你马上接。录你的四句。

1. Alex：*I think we should present the results on Tuesday.*  
   你：*Absolutely. And I think we should also prepare a backup slide.*  
2. Alex：*We can include the latest numbers too.*  
   你：*That's a good point. We should also consider the timeline.*  
3. Alex：*The timeline should be fine if we start Monday morning.*  
   你：*Just to add to that, we might need an extra hour for the review.*  
4. Alex：*Let me check with the team about…*  
   你：*Sorry to interrupt. I have a quick question. Did we confirm the room?*  
   然后：*Could I jump in here? I can send the request now.*

三类都要出现：同意、补充、打断。

### C｜迁移（选做）

换成周末出门。各说一句。

1. *Absolutely. And I think we should leave in the morning.*  
2. *Just to add to that, we should book the hotel tonight.*  
3. *Could I jump in? Does anyone have a car?*

## 自检

**1. *Could I jump in?* 句尾怎么走？**

A. 往下，像在陈述  
B. 往上，像在问能不能说

答案：**B**。这是在征求空档。

**2. 开口**

> Absolutely. And I think the design needs one more revision.  
> Sorry to interrupt. I have a quick question about the schedule.  
> /ˈæbsəluːtli ‖ ˌɪntəˈrʌpt/

*Absolutely* 重音在第一拍。*interrupt* 后面停一下再问。

**3. 是非**

你在会上只说 *Also, I want to add…* 就插进去了。

*Also* 太短，下一个人容易直接盖过去。改成 *Just to add to that,* 再接你的那一点。说一遍。
