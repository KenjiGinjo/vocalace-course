问候可以接 *Pretty good* 再加 *You?*，见 [6.1.1](/6-dialogue/6-1/hi/)。天气和周末比那一句长一点。你回了 *It was good*，然后沉默，对方只能再找话题。说完自己的答案，加一个细节，再把同一件事问回去。

问具体信息的句子往下收，是非问句往上，见 [3.2.1](/3-sentences/3-2/contour/)。*Yeah* 是 /jɛ/，见 [7.3.1](/7-expressions/7-3/yeah/)。

没有整句录音。how [🔊](/audio/words/how.mp3)、about [🔊](/audio/words/about.mp3)、pretty [🔊](/audio/words/pretty.mp3)、good [🔊](/audio/words/good.mp3)、much [🔊](/audio/words/much.mp3)、great [🔊](/audio/words/great.mp3)、out [🔊](/audio/words/out.mp3) 只对单词。

这课目标：天气或周末，你的话里要有回答、一个细节、以及 *How about you?* *Not much* 后面不能停。

## 口型

| 话题 | 你听到或你先说 | 你接 |
|------|----------------|------|
| 天气 | Nice weather today, huh? | Yeah, it's great. + 一件小事. Did you get to go outside? |
| 天气，没那么好 | — | It's kind of cloudy out. |
| 周末 | How was your weekend? | It was pretty good. I + 做了什么. How about you? |
| 周末很平淡 | What did you do? | Not much. Just + 一件小事. How about you? |

*huh* 往上，是在请对方接，不是在出题。*How was your weekend?* 是在问情况，句尾往下。*Do anything fun?* 是是非问句，*fun* 往上。

*Not much* 等于「没什么特别的」。后面要有 *Just relaxed* 这类一句，或直接 *How about you?*

## 对比词

| 说法 | 音 |
|------|----|
| weather | /ˈwɛðɚ/ |
| huh | /hʌ/ |
| pretty good | /ˈprɪt̬i ɡʊd/ |
| How about you? | /haʊ əˈbaʊt ju/ |
| outside | /ˌaʊtˈsaɪd/ |
| Not much. | /nɑːt mʌtʃ/ |

*weather* 中间是 /ð/，词尾 /ɚ/。*pretty* 中间是闪音，见 [1.7.1](/1-phonetics/1-7/flap/)。pretty [🔊](/audio/words/pretty.mp3)。*good* 是短的 /ɡʊd/。good [🔊](/audio/words/good.mp3)。不要把元音拉长。

*how* 单独念是 /haʊ/。how [🔊](/audio/words/how.mp3)。*about* 重音在第二拍。about [🔊](/audio/words/about.mp3) /əˈbaʊt/。*great* 是 /ɡreɪt/。great [🔊](/audio/words/great.mp3)。

*out* 是 /aʊt/。out [🔊](/audio/words/out.mp3)。*outside* 重音在 *side*。*much* 是 /mʌtʃ/。much [🔊](/audio/words/much.mp3)。

*kind of* 快了听起来像 *kinda*，见 [3.1.3](/3-sentences/3-1/reduced/)。这课仍说 *kind of*。

## 放进句子

**句 1：**

> **Nice** weather today, **huh**?  
> /naɪs ˈwɛðɚ təˈdeɪ hʌ/  
> 今天天气不错吧？

你接：*Yeah, it's great. I almost went for a walk at lunch. Did you get to go outside at all?*

**句 2：**

> It was **pretty good**. I just stayed in and watched TV. **How about you?** Do anything **fun**?  
> /ɪt wəz ˈprɪt̬i ɡʊd ‖ aɪ dʒʌst steɪd ɪn ən wɑːtʃt tiːˈviː ‖ haʊ əˈbaʊt ju ‖ du ˈɛniθɪŋ fʌn/  
> 挺好的。我就待在家里看电视。你呢？有没有做什么好玩的？

*How about you* 连着说。*fun* 清楚，往上。

平淡的周末：*Not much. Just relaxed at home. How about you?*

## 常见错误

**1. 答完就停**  
每轮数自己的句子。只有评价、没有后面那句，就补上 *How about you?* 再停。

**2. 用 And you? 把话抛回去**  
问候课里的 *You?* 仍接在 *What's up* 后面。天气和周末这课用 *How about you?*

**3. It was good 三个词一样平**  
加上 *pretty*，*good* 仍是短的 /ɡʊd/。细节放在下一小句，不要靠把 *good* 拉长。

## 练习

### A｜跟读（约 4 分钟）

下面两组各说两遍，录周末那组。回听有没有 *How about you*。

- Nice weather today, huh?
- Yeah, it's great. I almost went for a walk at lunch. Did you get to go outside at all?
- How was your weekend?
- It was pretty good. I just stayed in and watched TV. How about you? Do anything fun?

过关：能指出接球那句在不在，*fun* 往不往上。

### B｜开口（约 4 分钟）

用你真实的一个周末活动，说完整三句，录下来。只听接球句。没有就只补最后一句。

*How was your weekend?*  
*It was pretty good. I [went to the gym / had dinner with friends / watched a game].*  
*How about you? Do anything fun?*

### C｜迁移（选做）

换成早上。*How's your morning?* *Pretty busy. I have two meetings. How about you?* 说一遍。

## 自检

**1. 朋友问周末怎么样，你说了 *It was good*，对方没接。下一句加什么？**

A. 把 good 换成 great，然后停  
B. I went hiking. How about you?  
C. 不说话，等对方再问

答案：**B**。细节加上把话抛回去。

**2. 开口**

> How was your weekend? It was pretty good. I just stayed in. How about you? Do anything fun?  
> /ˈprɪt̬i ɡʊd ‖ haʊ əˈbaʊt ju ‖ fʌn/

*pretty* 要在。*fun* 往上。

**3. 是非**

你说 *Not much* 就停了。

补上 *Just relaxed at home. How about you?* 再录这一段。
